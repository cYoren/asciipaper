Community rules

Every look is reviewed before it appears in the Community. These rules decide what gets approved.

Welcome

  • Original looks and scenes you made, or remixed and changed.
  • Calm, beautiful, strange or funny wallpapers. Clear titles that say what it is.

Not allowed

  • Sexual content, nudity, graphic violence or gore.
  • Hate, harassment, threats, or content that targets a person or group.
  • Rapid flashing or strobing that could trigger seizures.
  • Other people's logos, characters or artwork you have no right to share.
  • Personal information, links, ads, spam, or titles meant to mislead.
  • Scenes built to slow down or crash devices.

Reporting and blocking

Use Report on any look that breaks these rules. Looks with several reports are hidden for everyone until we review them. Use Block to stop seeing anything from that author.

What we do

We remove looks that break these rules. To appeal or report something urgent, email contact@medhub.one.
