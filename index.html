Live wallpapers that move with your mouse. One click turns them into LEGO, pixel art, CRT glass, Game Boy or Matrix code, and any picture or video you love becomes one too.

60 styles, one click

Every wallpaper takes any style, then you fine-tune it live: 13 shapes, 21 character sets, 20 retro palettes, dithering, and effects you can stack: CRT curve, scanlines, bloom, RGB split, grain, glitch.

Make it yours

Port anything

Drop in a photo, a GIF, a video or a link to a post on X. asciipaper measures it and picks a great starting look.

Share a look in one line

Every look is a short code. Paste a friend's code and you get their exact style. Pictures you save carry their look inside.

Big text

Your name, a word, a quote: drawn in characters, rippling under your pointer, in any style.

Let AI design one

Ask Claude for "a slow teal aurora, CRT style". It writes the wallpaper, looks at each attempt, and sets the one you like.

Light on your PC

A tiny native engine draws on the GPU at under 1% of a CPU core, and pauses behind fullscreen windows.

Write your own

A wallpaper is one GLSL function. Save the file and it updates on your desktop as you type.
